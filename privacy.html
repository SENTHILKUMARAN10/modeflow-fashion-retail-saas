Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

ModeFlow is a retail management service for businesses. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the service.

Information we process

ModeFlow may process account information such as name and email address, business information, products, inventory, customers, sales, expenses and related operational records that you choose to enter.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, provide the retail management service, store and synchronize business records, protect accounts, troubleshoot problems and improve reliability.

Data separation

Business records are associated with a business workspace and access is controlled through authenticated membership and database row-level security. Users should only access workspaces they are authorized to use.

Service providers

ModeFlow uses third-party infrastructure, including Supabase for authentication/database services and GitHub Pages for frontend hosting. Their own privacy and security practices also apply.

Retention and deletion

Business data is retained while an account or workspace remains active unless deletion is requested or required. Backup copies may remain temporarily as part of normal recovery processes.

Your responsibilities

Businesses using ModeFlow are responsible for entering customer information lawfully, providing any required notices to their customers, and limiting access to authorized staff.

Security

ModeFlow uses authenticated access, HTTPS, database row-level security and role-based controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy or account questions, contact the ModeFlow operator through the support details provided when you subscribe.
