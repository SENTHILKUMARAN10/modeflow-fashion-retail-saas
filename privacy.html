Privacy policy.

This policy explains how Salesventory handles account, workspace and business information when you use the service.

Information we process

Salesventory may process account information, business workspace details, products or services, customers, sales, invoices, expenses and related operational data that you choose to enter.

How information is used

Information is used to provide Salesventory, secure accounts, operate business workspaces, generate invoices and reports, maintain reliability and support users.

Workspace access and security

Business records are associated with a workspace and access is intended to be limited to authorized members. Salesventory uses authenticated access and database access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Service providers

Salesventory may rely on third-party infrastructure, authentication, hosting and payment providers to operate the service. Those providers process information as required to deliver their services.

Customer information

Businesses using Salesventory are responsible for collecting and using their own customer information lawfully and for giving any notices required by applicable law.

Retention and requests

Business data may be retained while an account or workspace remains active and for a reasonable period where required for security, recovery or legal obligations. For privacy or account questions, contact support@Salesventory.app.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Salesventory evolves. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
