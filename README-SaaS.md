# Thatha Kadai — Business Billing & Inventory SaaS

A portfolio-focused redesign of a real-world Sunday meat-shop billing system into a modern small-business SaaS product.

## Problem
Small local businesses often manage billing, inventory, expenses and customer details separately. This project combines those workflows into one responsive dashboard.

## Core Features
- KPI dashboard for revenue, orders, stock value and customers
- Invoice and billing workflow
- Searchable recent transactions
- Inventory health and stock usage tracking
- Low-stock forecast messaging
- Customer management
- Expense tracking
- Sales and business reports
- Responsive desktop/mobile interface

## Tech Stack
