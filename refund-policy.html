Refund policy.

This page explains how payment issues and refund requests for Velora subscriptions are handled.

Subscription payments

Velora subscriptions are billed according to the plan and billing period selected at checkout. Once a paid subscription has been activated, fees are generally not refundable for unused time except where required by applicable law.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you believe you were charged more than once, charged the wrong amount, or a payment completed but your subscription was not activated correctly, contact support so the transaction can be reviewed.

Technical payment failures

Where a payment is confirmed as collected but the service cannot be delivered because of a verified technical issue, Velora may provide account credit, restore the subscription period or issue a refund as appropriate.

How to request a review

Contact support@velora.app with the account email, payment date, amount and transaction reference. Do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials.

Processing time

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method where possible. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary after a refund is issued.

Legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits refund or consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded in your jurisdiction.
